Press /cherry and hit Enter — jumps forward to the first match after your cursor. New move unlocked: / searches forward through the file.

Press n — jumps to the next match. Press it again if there's another one further down. n always repeats your last search in the same direction you started it.

Press ?cherry and hit Enter instead — searches BACKWARD from your cursor instead of forward. New move unlocked: ? is /'s mirror image.

Press N now — it repeats your last search, but backward this time, the opposite of whatever n would do. n and N are always relative to your original search direction, not fixed to forward/backward themselves.
