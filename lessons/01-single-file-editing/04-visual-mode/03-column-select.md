Cursor on the "a" of "apple" above, press Ctrl-v, then j twice to drag a rectangular selection down through all three lines. New move unlocked: Ctrl-v selects a column of characters, not whole lines or a single stretch of text.

Still selected? Press $ — it extends the selection to the true end of EACH line, even though "banana pie" is longer than the others. Press d to delete that ragged rectangle in one combo.

Same trick works from the other end: Ctrl-v the first column of those lines again, press $ to reach each line's real end, then A, type something, then jk — appends to every selected line simultaneously.
