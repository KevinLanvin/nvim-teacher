Welcome to Campaign 2: Write. Four doors lead into insert mode, and this level teaches all four of them.

Press i right before this word →banana, type your name, then jk to get back to normal mode. New move unlocked: i inserts BEFORE the cursor. jk is how you always leave insert mode in this config.

a is like i but inserts AFTER the cursor instead of before. Try it on the same word, then jk again.

I is the line-wide version of i: no matter where your cursor sits on a line, I jumps you to the first real character and drops you into insert mode there. Try it anywhere on this line, type something, then jk.

A is the line-wide version of a: it jumps to the very end of the line and starts inserting there, cursor position doesn't matter. Try it on this line too — type something at the end, then jk.
